## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **startup validation and venture investment feasibility analysis** for **AI Career Copilot**, an AI-powered platform designed to streamline the career preparation journey for engineering students and early-career professionals. The report evaluates the startup's market potential, competitive landscape, business model, customer segments, risks, and growth strategy using a venture capital perspective.
